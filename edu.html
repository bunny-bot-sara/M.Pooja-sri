📖 Learning & Study Tips

Small habits can make learning easier.

📌 Active Reading: Take notes while reading.
🔁 Spaced Repetition: Review important topics at intervals.
👩‍🏫 Teach Others: Explain a topic to someone else to strengthen understanding.
✍️ Practice Problems: Apply concepts instead of only reading them.
⏰ Time Management: Study in focused 25-minute blocks and take regular breaks.
🎯 Exam Preparation: Review previous papers, formulas and mock tests.
